# Rage bar

In Bear Form, your rage in a bar of its own, near your character instead of up in the corner. The fill shows how much you have, the number sits on it, and the bar lights up when you are at full rage.

## What it does

- **The bar.** A painted bar with a stone and bear claws on the end. The red fill grows with your rage, and a glowing line marks its end.
- **Bear Form only.** The bar never shows in caster form, Cat Form or travel forms.
- **The number** of rage points sits on the bar. You can turn it off.
- **Full rage.** At 100 the whole bar glows.
- **When it shows.** In Bear Form, while you have rage and the whole time you are in combat. Turn on **Always show the bar** to keep it up while you are a bear.
- **Horizontal or vertical.** Vertical stands the bar up with the claws at the bottom, and the fill grows upward.
- **Your size and place.** Unlock the frames to drag the bar where you want it, and scroll on it to make it bigger or smaller.
- **Skins.** In the Dream skin you get the painted bar. The Blizzard skin gives a plain bar with the game's bar texture, and the Flat skin a plain bar in flat colours.

## How to use it

1. Nothing to set up: the bar is on by default, below the middle of the screen. Shift into Bear Form.
2. Want it upright? Turn on **Vertical** on the **Rage bar** page.
3. Want it somewhere else? Click **Unlock frames** (or type `/dream unlock`), drag the bar, then lock the frames again.

## Settings

In `/dream`, page **Rage bar**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the rage bar | On | Turns the bar on or off. |
| Vertical | Off | Stands the bar up, filling from the bottom. |
| Show the number | On | The rage points on the bar. |
| Always show the bar | Off | Keeps the bar up in Bear Form out of combat with no rage too. |

## Good to know

**The full glow doesn't show in combat.**
WoW: Forever hides some combat values from addons. If it hides your rage, the bar still fills and shows the number (the game draws them), but Dreambound can't tell when you are full.
