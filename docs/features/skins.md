# Skins

Dreambound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Dream** (default) | Deep green-teal panels with a soft green edge, a drifting mist, leaves in the corners and a faint Dreambound crest behind each card. A card that needs you has a slow pale glow at its edge. | The default UI, or if you like the druid look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own grey and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons. No crest on the cards. | A mostly default UI. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark grey, Arial Narrow text, no crest. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

1. Type `/dream` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Dreambound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

The warning color, for a buff that is missing or a reagent at 0, stands out in every skin.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Dreambound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.
