# Travel panel

Press Escape and a small **Travel** panel opens beside the game menu, with Teleport: Moonglade, Travel Form, Aquatic Form and your Hearthstone. Click one to cast it.

## What it does

- The panel shows whenever the game menu opens, out of combat.
- Three spells are listed under **DRUID**:

| Row | Before you learn it | Where to learn it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teleport: Moonglade | level 10 | a druid trainer |
| Travel Form | level 30 | a druid trainer |
| Aquatic Form | level 16 | the druid quest for it |

- Before you learn a spell, its row is greyed out and shows the level. Its tooltip says where to learn it.
- **Hearthstone** is listed below, with the inn it is set to, for example `Hearthstone: Lion's Pride Inn`. The row only shows while you carry a Hearthstone.
- Click a row to cast it. The game menu closes after the click.

## How to use it

1. Press Escape.
2. Click a row in the panel.

## Settings

In `/dream`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Turns the panel on or off. |

## Good to know

**Not in combat.**
The panel only opens out of combat, and it hides when a fight starts. Spells cast from it need you out of combat anyway.

**Can I move the panel?**
No. It always sits to the right of the game menu.

**How do I get back from Moonglade?**
Use your Hearthstone or the flight master there.
