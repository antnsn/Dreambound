# Combo points

In Cat Form your finishers get stronger with each combo point. Dreambound shows them as five claws, so you see at a glance when it is time for Ferocious Bite or Rip.

## What it does

- **Five claws.** One lights up, with a warm glow round it, for each combo point on your target. The rest are dark.
- **Cat Form only.** The bar never shows in caster form, Bear Form or travel forms, not even in combat.
- **When it shows.** In Cat Form, while you have combo points and the whole time you are in combat. Out of combat with no points, it hides. Turn on **Always show the bar** to keep it up while you are a cat.
- **Flash at 5.** When you reach 5 points, the claws flash a few times: time for a finisher. They flash once each time you reach 5, not again while you stay there.
- **Your size and place.** Unlock the frames to drag the bar where you want it, and scroll on it to make it bigger or smaller.

## How to use it

1. Nothing to set up: the bar is on by default, below the middle of the screen. Shift into Cat Form and hit something.
2. Want it somewhere else? Click **Unlock frames** (or type `/dream unlock`), drag the bar, then lock the frames again.

## Settings

In `/dream`, page **Combo points**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the combo point bar | On | Turns the bar on or off. |
| Flash at 5 points | On | The claws flash a few times when you reach 5 points. |
| Always show the bar | Off | Keeps the bar up in Cat Form out of combat with no points too. |

## Good to know

**The bar stays hidden in combat.**
WoW: Forever hides some combat values from addons. If it hides combo points, Dreambound hides the bar rather than show a wrong count. Tell us if that happens to you.

**The Blizzard and Flat skins.**
The painted claws belong to the Dream skin. The Blizzard and Flat skins show five plain squares instead, lit in the same colour.
