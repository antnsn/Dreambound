# Explanations

Hover over a Dreambound card or a travel panel row and the tooltip says, in a sentence or two, what it is for. Turn it off once you know it all.

## What it does

The explanations show in the tooltips of:

- the three [buff cards](buffs.html), also when a card offers a cast (the tooltip then names the spell and ends with **Click to cast (out of combat)**),
- the [reagents card](reagents.html),
- the [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) cards,
- the Teleport: Moonglade, Travel Form, Aquatic Form and Hearthstone rows of the [travel panel](travel.html).

The texts:

| Where | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Mark of the Wild card | Mark of the Wild gives armor, all stats and resistances. Keep it on yourself and your group. Gift of the Wild counts. |
| Thorns card | Thorns hurts anything that hits you in melee. Keep it up while you level or tank. |
| Omen of Clarity card | Omen of Clarity (a talent) sometimes makes your next spell or ability free. Keep it up all the time. |
| Reagents card | Gift of the Wild buffs your whole group at once and uses Wild Berries (rank 1) or Wild Thornroot (rank 2), sold by reagent vendors. Rebirth brings a dead player back to life, even in combat. Each rank uses its own seed (Maple, Stranglethorn, Ashwood, Hornbeam, Ironwood), sold by reagent vendors. |
| Well-Rested card | Sleeping in a Cozy Sleeping Bag gives Well-Rested: more experience for a while. |
| Well Fed card | Eating buff food gives Well Fed: better stats, and on Forever more experience too. |
| Teleport: Moonglade (travel panel) | Teleport: Moonglade takes you to Moonglade, where druids learn some of their spells. Use your Hearthstone or the flight master to leave. |
| Travel Form (travel panel) | Travel Form makes you run 40% faster outdoors. You can't attack or cast spells in it. |
| Aquatic Form (travel panel) | Aquatic Form lets you swim faster and breathe under water. |
| Hearthstone (travel panel) | Your Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to. Talk to an innkeeper to change it. |

## How to use it

Hover over a card or a row. That's all.

## Settings

In `/dream`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Shows the explanations. Off: no explanations; a card that offers a cast still shows the spell name and "Click to cast", and a travel row still shows its name and whether you can cast it. |

`/dream explain` turns it on or off from chat.

## Good to know

**No tooltip while frames are unlocked.**
While you move frames, the cards don't show their explanations, so they don't get in the way.
