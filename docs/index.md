## Why Dreambound exists

A druid keeps a few long buffs up: Mark of the Wild, Thorns, and Omen of Clarity if you have the talent. Each lasts long enough to forget. Gift of the Wild needs Wild Berries or Wild Thornroot, and Rebirth needs a seed, and both run out at the wrong moment.

Dreambound watches all of that. It shows a card when a buff is missing or about to run out, and you click the card to cast it. It warns you before your berries, thornroot or seeds run out. One key buffs your group with Mark of the Wild. It only loads on druids.

## What it does

- **Buff cards.** A card when Mark of the Wild, Thorns or Omen of Clarity is missing or running out. Click it to cast. Gift of the Wild counts as Mark of the Wild. [More](features/buffs.html)
- **Buff key.** Out of combat, each press casts what a card asks for, then Mark of the Wild on the next group member in range who is missing it. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Reagents.** A card when Wild Berries, Wild Thornroot or your Rebirth seeds run low. [More](features/reagents.html)
- **Combo points.** In Cat Form, five claws, one lit for each combo point on your target, with a flash at 5. [More](features/combo-points.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: Teleport: Moonglade, Travel Form, Aquatic Form and your Hearthstone sit beside the game menu. [More](features/travel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations.** Hover over a card or button to read what it does and why. [More](features/explanations.html)
- **Your layout.** Every frame can be moved, snapped to a grid and resized. [More](features/frames-and-minimap.html)
- **Three skins.** Dream, Blizzard and Flat. [More](features/skins.html)

## The Bound family

Dreambound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core: Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest), Felbound (warlock), Dreambound (druid), Ragebound (warrior) and Shadowbound (rogue). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
